1. Creatio: 70% of enterprises are past AI pilots, but only 29% have full governance

Creatio's second annual State of Enterprise AI Adoption report, released on 20 October and based on 600+ IT and business decision-makers, finds about 70% of organisations beyond experimentation: 31% have AI deployed in selected functions, 25% are scaling across the organisation and 14% have it embedded enterprise-wide. Only 9% of respondents expect AI agents to cut headcount significantly, while 89% expect them to add capacity, augment teams or create roles. Governance is the weak spot, with just 29% reporting fully established AI governance and 44% partial governance. Firms automating more than half their workflows were twice as likely to report revenue gains than those automating under 10%, though this is vendor-run research and the full report sits behind a registration form.

Sources: Creatio, TechIntelPro, Yahoo Finance, VMblog

2. Daytona raises $60M at a $1B valuation to build a cloud for AI agents

Daytona, which sells isolated, elastic sandboxes where AI agents can run code and use tools, announced a $60 million Series B on 20 October at a $1 billion valuation. FirstMark led, with angels including Patrick Collison, Tobi Lütke, Aravind Srinivas, Adam D'Angelo and Eric Yuan, and strategic backing from Qualcomm Ventures, Samsung Next and Okta Ventures; total funding is now $91 million. One aggregator reports run-rate revenue up roughly 25x between January and September, a figure we could not verify against the company. For businesses, the round is a marker that the plumbing needed to run agents safely at scale is becoming its own funded infrastructure layer.

Sources: Tech.eu, Dealroom, The Recursive, AI Weekly

3. Motive launches Agents that act on fleet alerts, from calling drivers to opening work orders

Motive announced Agents on 19 October, showcased at the ATA Management Conference, an AI layer that turns fleet safety and maintenance alerts into actions rather than dashboards to check. When a fault code meets an operator's criteria, an Agent can explain the issue and severity, bring in Motive's Atlas assistant to talk the driver through a safe response, open a work order in Motive Maintenance and mark the vehicle out of service. Teams start from guided workflows or templates without technical expertise, and a plain-language Agent builder is promised early next year. Coverage differs on how much of the in-cab calling is live today versus later this year, so treat that part as still rolling out.

Sources: Motive, Heavy Duty Trucking, CCJ, FleetOwner

4. Demandbase launches three agents, including one that tracks how AI assistants describe your brand

At its GO conference in New York on 21 October, Demandbase launched an AEO Agent, a Campaign Agent and an Audience Agent, framing them as an extension of its agentic strategy into business-to-agent (B2A) marketing. The AEO Agent monitors how AI assistants describe a brand, down to individual members of a buying group, lets marketers compare representations across major assistants and shows which sources shape the answers. The premise is that AI recommendations now influence which vendors buyers shortlist. The agents build on Mojo, the campaign agent Demandbase introduced in September for Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads and Salesforce.

Sources: DestinationCRM, MarTech Edge, Demand Gen Report

5. Azoma raises $11.2M to help brands get recommended by AI shopping agents

London-based Azoma closed an $11.2 million Series A on 21 October, led by Puma Growth Partners with participation from dunnhumby, Twin Path, MaRS IAF and others. Its Agentic Commerce Optimization platform tracks and improves how brands are surfaced when AI assistants such as ChatGPT Dots and Meta Muse recommend products, and the new money funds expansion across those assistants plus attribution tools for enterprise brands using dunnhumby data. Customers include L'Oréal, Unilever, Mars, Beiersdorf and Reckitt, and revenue grew almost tenfold over the past year. One outlet reports the round as £8 million, but most sources use the dollar figure.

Sources: SaaS Rise, Startup Researcher, Tech Startups, Runtime Wire
