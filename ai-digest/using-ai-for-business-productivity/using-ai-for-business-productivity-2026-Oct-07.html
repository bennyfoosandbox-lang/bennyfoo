1. OpenAI Marketplace lets enterprises spend their OpenAI commitment on partner products, starting with Baseten's open models

OpenAI's B2B Marketplace, announced around DevDay in early October, lets eligible enterprise customers apply part of an existing OpenAI commitment to partner products instead of signing separate contracts. Baseten is one of the first partners, so open-source models it serves can be used inside Codex or through the Responses API and billed against that commitment. Baseten says it runs inference on US infrastructure, doesn't store prompts, and supports regional deployment and per-model, per-user usage reporting. For buyers, this makes it simpler to route each task to a closed or open model within one budget and one procurement relationship.

Sources: Digital Today, Digg, OpenAI Developer Community

2. Albertsons widens its OpenAI deal from a Safeway shopping plug-in to ChatGPT Enterprise across its workforce

Albertsons, which runs more than 2,200 stores serving about 36 million customers a week, is expanding its work with OpenAI beyond the customer-facing Safeway experience in ChatGPT. Select internal teams are now using ChatGPT Enterprise and the OpenAI API to streamline work, speed up decisions, build promotional insights and find practices that can scale. The company also plans to bring the ChatGPT shopping flow to its other banners beyond Safeway. It is a concrete retail example of one vendor relationship covering both a customer channel and back-office productivity.

Sources: OpenAI, Chain Store Age

3. ADP's People at Work 2026: half of workers use AI weekly, yet daily users are four times likelier to feel less productive

ADP Research's People at Work 2026 survey, covering more than 39,000 workers in 36 markets, finds that half of workers use AI several times a week and one in five uses it nearly every day. Frequent users report higher engagement and lower negative stress than non-users, but daily users are also four times more likely to say they feel less productive, with only 30% of daily users reporting full engagement. ADP's reading is that as checklist work moves to AI and people shift to longer, strategic projects, existing productivity metrics may no longer capture the value. This is a measurement warning for any rollout judged on activity or hours saved.

Sources: ADP Research (Australia), Enterprise DNA, ADP Research

4. Claude Code Projects: one always-on conversation that fans work out to parallel cloud threads (from Sep 17)

Anthropic's Claude Code Projects entered beta on 17 September for a subset of Pro and Max subscribers. It is a single persistent conversation that remembers the project goal and decides whether to start a new thread or hand a request to one already working on that area. Each thread is a cloud session with its own branch and repository copy, so work continues with the laptop closed and can be followed from a phone through an overview panel. This item is about three weeks old, included because the 24-hour window had too few new items. It signals a shift from prompting one agent to supervising a coordinated team.

Sources: VentureBeat, Pasquale Pillitteri, Ecosistema Startup

5. EU Digital Omnibus pushes high-risk AI Act duties to December 2027, giving enterprise agent deployments more runway

The EU's Digital Omnibus agreement defers obligations for stand-alone high-risk AI systems listed in Annex III to 2 December 2027, and for AI embedded in regulated products to 2 August 2028. Article 50 transparency and watermarking duties still applied to new systems from 2 August 2026, with a later date for systems already on the market. Law-firm and security-research commentary stresses that the Act was drafted around assistive AI, so autonomous agents that execute decisions sit awkwardly in its risk categories. One analysis says agent deployments outpace governance by about 14 months. The reprieve is breathing room for governance work, not a reason to pause it. The exact agreement date was not verified from the sources.

Sources: Gibson Dunn, Travers Smith, Cloud Security Alliance
