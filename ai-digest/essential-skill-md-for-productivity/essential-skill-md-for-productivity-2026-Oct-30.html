1. Claude for Nonprofits gets a plugin of ready-made skills

Anthropic's Oct 26 post adds a Claude for Nonprofits plugin: a set of skills and connectors built with about 30 nonprofits. The skills cover marketing (campaign planning, drafting in the organisation's voice), hiring (job posts, interview guides, 90-day onboarding plans), board work, and fundraising (funder research, RFP eligibility breakdowns, proposal and report drafting, donor pre-call briefs). Two skills do setup: one maps where the organisation keeps donor, grant and finance records so the others know where to look, and one checks requests against the organisation's AI policy. It installs automatically at no extra cost on Team and Enterprise nonprofit plans. Anthropic says more than 25,000 nonprofits use Claude, and over 75% of them have fewer than 10 seats.

Sources: Anthropic (Claude blog), Anthropic, Data Studios, AI Catchup

2. Claude for Financial Advisors grows to 22 connectors and eight workflow skills

Anthropic's Financial Advisors plugin first launched Sep 14, and its page was updated Oct 27 to add more partner connectors, which is why it counts here as a material update. It now lists 22 connectors, including Addepar, Charles Schwab, Orion, BlackRock, Vanguard, J.P. Morgan (55ip), FactSet and iCapital. The eight named skills are advisor onboarding, alternative investments brief, compliance and AI policy, estate and tax brief, portfolio rebalance review, post-meeting notes and follow-up, pre-meeting prep, and prospect intake. Anything client-facing or regulated needs advisor approval. Anthropic recommends Enterprise plans for registered investment advisers because of the audit logs, and the skills are also in its GitHub skill repository to adopt or adapt.

Sources: Anthropic (Claude blog), Unite.AI, WealthManagement.com, Futurum

3. alirezarezvani/claude-skills: a 29k-star pack with a dedicated productivity domain

This community repo now reports 401 skills across 20 domains and about 29,000 GitHub stars, though its own README gives inconsistent counts (380, 401 and 345). Its productivity domain lists 12 skills, including capture (brain dump to actions), inbox setup and triage, weekly review with a refusal gate, deep-work time-blocking, and a meetings skill with a cost gate, agenda and action items. A handoff skill is inspired by Matt Pocock's. The README marks a Research Operations domain (v2.9.0), Gemini CLI support and multi-tool conversion as new. The page shows no dates for these changes, so treat the freshness as unconfirmed.

Sources: GitHub: alirezarezvani/claude-skills, GitHub: CLAUDE.md

4. SkillMedev/skills publishes 482 MIT-licensed SKILL.md files, many aimed at productivity

The open Skill Me catalog says its repo holds 482 portable SKILL.md files under an MIT license, while its site claims 2,500+ skills and packs. The productivity-leaning set includes Inbox Zero, Email Triage, Weekly Review, Deep Work Planner, Meeting Notes to Actions, OKR Builder, Getting Things Done and Second Brain. Install paths are a connector for Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or Cursor, an npx CLI, the skills CLI, or copying a file into .claude/skills, .cursor/skills or .agents/skills. Caution: the repo has only 20 stars and 7 commits, so it is early and unvetted, and given the supply-chain problems covered earlier you should read any skill before installing it.

Sources: GitHub: SkillMedev/skills
