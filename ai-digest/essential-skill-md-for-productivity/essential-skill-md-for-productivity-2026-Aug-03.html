1. OpenClaw closes a skill-sandbox escape with realpath() containment

OpenClaw's rolling 2026.7.2 release train — betas landing through August 2 — quietly shipped a security fix specifically for skills: every skill file now passes through realpath() validation to confirm it actually resolves inside its declared root directory before OpenClaw will load it. That closes off a path-traversal-style trick a poorly-vetted or malicious SKILL.md could otherwise use to reach files outside its intended sandbox. It ships alongside a broader reliability push in the same release — a crash-recoverable SQLite quarantine store, durable channel delivery, and session rewind — that reads as a direct response to the ClawHavoc and SkillCloak-style findings this digest has tracked through the summer. It's a small, unglamorous line item, but it's exactly the "does the runtime actually enforce the sandbox" fix the skill-security research covered here keeps calling for.

Sources: GitHub — openclaw/openclaw releases, Releasebot — OpenClaw updates, NewReleases.io

2. Tencent's SkillHub launches SkillPay, wiring WeChat Pay directly into skill invocation

Tencent's SkillHub — the China-focused, OpenClaw-based AI skills community it launched in March — rolled out SkillPay in mid-July, a commercialization layer that lets merchants list, price, and get paid for AI agent skills, with WeChat Pay handling user authorization and settlement. It's a distinct product from the Tencent Cloud ADP 4.0 enterprise skills marketplace already covered here (July 27): SkillHub/SkillPay targets individual and small-merchant creators selling consumer-facing skills, folding listing, agent invocation, and payment into a single flow rather than an enterprise Smart Desk bundle. It's a concrete answer to a question this digest keeps circling back to — how skill authors actually get paid — and one of the first mainstream examples wiring a consumer payment rail directly into skill invocation rather than routing through a Stripe-style marketplace cut.

Sources: Dealroom.co, Longbridge News Recap, KuCoin News
